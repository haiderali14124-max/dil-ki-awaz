01 / FEELINGS

The Silent Heart

Some feelings have no sound, some tears never fall. The heart keeps its own language, even when we say nothing at all.

02 / DISTANCE

Close, Even Far Away

Distance may divide the roads, and time may change the view. But some people stay within the heart, no matter how far from you.

One More Sunrise

When yesterday feels heavy, let tomorrow softly start. Every sunrise brings a chance to make peace with your heart.
